DSGN 230 — Survey of Design History

All images in the Digital Revolution & Contemporary Design module are used for educational purposes in the context of a university survey course on design history. Free-license images will be credited to their photographers under the terms of their respective Creative Commons or public-domain licenses. Non-free material (product screenshots, platform interfaces, promotional imagery, trademarked logos, or AI-generated installation documentation) is reproduced at low resolution for educational identification under fair use and is not released under a free license by this course.

This page documents the image slots that have been planned for this module. Where a slot is marked "pending," an image has not yet been added to the module folder, and the attribution will be filled in when a specific image is selected.

About these credits

This module documents the digital era of design, a period in which the primary artifacts are screens of running software, proprietary platforms, and copyrighted corporate interfaces. Unlike earlier eras, where photographs of buildings or furniture frequently circulate under Creative Commons licenses, much of the visual record of the digital era is owned by the companies that produced the interfaces. This credits page will be updated as specific images are selected and attributed, and fair-use reproductions will be clearly marked.

Wherever a photographer and a Creative Commons license are listed, the image is being used in accordance with that license. Entries marked "pending" indicate image slots for which a specific source has not yet been selected. If you are a rights holder or subject of a portrait and wish to correct or update an attribution on this page, please contact the course instructor.
