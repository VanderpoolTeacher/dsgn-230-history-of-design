About these credits

Wherever a photographer and a Creative Commons license are listed, the image is being used in accordance with that license. CC BY-SA licenses require attribution and share-alike terms; CC BY licenses require attribution. Public domain images (such as the Carol M. Highsmith Archive at the Library of Congress) have no usage restrictions. Entries marked "pending confirmation" use images routed through Wikimedia Commons whose specific upload metadata has not yet been matched to this credits page; those attributions will be updated as the image set is finalized.

If you are a photographer or rights holder and wish to correct or update an attribution on this page, please contact the course instructor.
