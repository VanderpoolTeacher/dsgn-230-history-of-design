## To add (8 images)

### Architecture

1. **`Vanna_Venturi_House.jpg`** — Robert Venturi, 1964, Chestnut Hill, PA
   - Source page: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanna_Venturi_House
   - The foundational work of postmodern architecture — the "mother's house."

2. **`Portland_Building.jpg`** — Michael Graves, 1982, Portland, OR
   - Source page: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland_Building
   - First major commission of built postmodern architecture.

3. **`ATT_Building.jpg`** — Philip Johnson & John Burgee, 1984, NYC
   - Source page: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/550_Madison_Avenue
   - The Chippendale-pediment skyscraper (now Sony Tower / 550 Madison).

4. **`Piazza_dItalia.jpg`** — Charles Moore, 1978, New Orleans, LA
   - Source page: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piazza_d%27Italia_(New_Orleans)
   - Pop-color classicism: neon columns, map of Italy in stone.

5. **`Neue_Staatsgalerie.jpg`** — James Stirling, 1984, Stuttgart, Germany
   - Source page: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neue_Staatsgalerie
   - European postmodernism's masterpiece: stone, steel, and saturated color.

### Product & Furniture

6. **`Sottsass_Carlton.jpg`** — Ettore Sottsass, Carlton Bookcase, 1981
   - Source page: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memphis_Group
   - The totemic Memphis Group object in laminate over particleboard.

### Theory & Graphic Design (harder to find freely licensed, optional)

7. **`Learning_From_Las_Vegas.jpg`** — Venturi / Scott Brown / Izenour book cover or "duck vs. decorated shed" diagram
   - Source page: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learning_from_Las_Vegas
   - Use any cover image or redrawn diagram that Wikipedia has available.

8. **`Wexner_Center.jpg`** — Peter Eisenman, 1989, Columbus, OH
   - Source page: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wexner_Center_for_the_Arts
   - The deconstructivist turn — grids colliding, walls cut away.
